HOW IT WORKS

Simple when every second matters.

Three steps that turn any thobe into a life-saving ID, without changing the way it looks or feels.

01

Tap or scan

An emergency helper taps the NFC tag or scans the QR code on the thobe with any modern phone — no app required.

02

See safe information

The emergency profile shows only the identity and contact details needed to help, keeping private data locked.

03

Contact family

One tap to share live location or dial the emergency contact, so family can be reached in seconds.

SECURITY BY DESIGN

Privacy comes first.

Sensitive information should never be exposed by a public QR scan. Smart Thobe splits the profile into layers, each with its own access rules.

🌐

Public layer

Minimal identity and emergency actions are visible to any helper who taps or scans — just enough to start helping.

🔐

Protected layer

Blood type, medical notes, and family details stay behind a PIN or authenticated access.
