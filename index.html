SECURITY BY DESIGN

Privacy comes first.

Sensitive information should never be exposed by a public QR scan. Smart Thobe splits the profile into layers, each with its own access rules.

🌐

Public layer

Minimal identity and emergency actions are visible to any helper who scans — just enough to start helping.

🔐

Protected layer

Blood type, medical notes, and family details stay behind a PIN or authenticated access.

👤

Owner control

The owner decides what is public, what is protected, and can update their information at any time.
